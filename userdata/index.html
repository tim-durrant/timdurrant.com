Data Deletion Statement

Last updated: September 2026

At Family Memory Assistant, we respect your right to privacy and maintain strict controls over your personal information. In accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), you have the right to request the permanent erasure of your data at any time.

How to Request Deletion of your Data:
To completely remove your data from our database, simply send an email to delete@timdurrant.com with your mobile number in the subject line and we will be in contact to confirm full erasure.

1. What Data is Removed?

After we confirm your request, we will review the data associated with your profile and arrange deletion of applicable Family Memory Assistant data from our active systems.

2. Processing Timeframes

After we confirm the request, we will process deletion of the applicable application data within a reasonable period and notify you when it is complete.

3. Limitations

Family Memory Assistant is responsible for data retained in the application systems that we control. This request does not delete messages retained by Meta, WhatsApp, email providers, or the user’s device. Those systems are subject to their own policies and retention practices.

4. Regulatory Compliance

The deletion process is designed to support our compliance obligations under APP 11.2 (Destruction or de-identification of personal information), ensuring that once your information is no longer needed to deliver our family assistance services, it is securely destroyed.
